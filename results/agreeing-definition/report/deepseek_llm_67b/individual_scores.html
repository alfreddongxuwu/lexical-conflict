No-rule baselines and F1 require Conventional; F2–F5 require Use Lexicon for asserted definitions. Tentative may_mean definitions retain both readings. Paired differences hold the reference fixed and do not measure instruction adherence. BLEU and chrF are divided by 100; COMET retains its native scale. English targets use chrF++ and default BLEU tokenization; Mandarin targets use character-only chrF and Chinese BLEU tokenization. Directions are reported separately. Blank metrics are excluded from means. Failed means the saved response fails the existing completion check (including empty responses); nonempty outputs remain scored as saved. Baseline EXPECTED means the expected word was found, not a human quality judgment. The all and baseline-label rows overlap; do not add their counts together. No significance tests are included. Conventional-baseline contrasts give each source item equal weight; higher/tied/lower counts there count source items. For comparisons between baseline conditions, intervals resample source items 2,000 times (seed 2026). Conflict contrasts have no intervals because targets are also reused. expected_word is the accepted conventional-word rate among jointly valid responses, on a 0–1 scale. Settings counts compare recorded generation arguments only, not weight identity or unrecorded defaults.
